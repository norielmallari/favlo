# Research

Three sections. Active models is the comparison table. Historical and Media are the catalog. One catalog row is one note. The note holds the table, the predecessors, and the command. Historical and Media do not restate them. Active models prints the four rate pairs. The note keeps the clocks, the command, and the search.

## Active models

Instruct, temperature 0, decode filled to 128, on an M5 Pro with 48 GB. Rates are tok/s, prefill then decode. A row is a protocol-complete `harness.synthetic` run: lengths 512, 1024, 4096, and 10000, a cold prefill, and the instruct card. 512 is the llama.cpp and MLX length. 1024 and 4096 are the oMLX lengths. 10000 is the hosted-API prompt length.

The model cell is `<id>, MTP off`, or `<id>, MTP on, depth <n>`. Weights is the loaded mix. KV is the resolved key/value plane. A new protocol run replaces the row with the same pipeline, weights, KV, and MTP label. A new weight mix or key/value plane replaces the previous Active row for that pipeline and MTP label, and the previous mix's note goes back to Historical. MTP on does not replace MTP off. A thinking-on run stays in Historical.
